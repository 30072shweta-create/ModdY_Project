# Pricing and Coupons

## Flight Pricing

The price of a flight can include different components based on the applicable pricing rules.

Pricing may consider factors such as:

* Base fare
* Taxes
* Additional fees
* Applicable discounts

## Cabin Classes

The system supports different cabin classes:

* Economy
* Premium Economy
* Business
* First Class

Flight pricing may vary depending on the selected cabin class.

## Pricing Rules

The system can apply different pricing rules based on conditions such as:

* Weekend travel
* Holidays
* Seasonal pricing
* Promotional offers

## Coupons

Users can apply eligible coupons to receive a discount on their booking.

A coupon may provide:

* A percentage-based discount
* A fixed amount discount

## Coupon Conditions

A coupon may have conditions such as:

* Active status
* Validity period
* Minimum booking amount
* Usage limits
* Maximum discount amount

A coupon must meet the required conditions before it can be applied.

## Final Price

The final price is calculated after considering the applicable pricing, fees, and eligible discounts.

The final price cannot be less than zero.
