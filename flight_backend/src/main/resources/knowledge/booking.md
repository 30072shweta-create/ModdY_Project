# Booking

## Creating a Booking

Users can create a booking after selecting a suitable flight.

The booking process involves selecting a flight and providing the required passenger details.

## Passenger Information

Passenger details are required when creating a booking.

A booking can include one or more passengers.

## Booking Confirmation

A booking is confirmed after the required booking and payment process is completed successfully.

## Booking Details

A booking contains information related to:

* Booking reference or booking code
* Selected flight or flights
* Passenger details
* Booking status
* Payment information

## Booking Status

A booking can move through different stages during its lifecycle, such as:

* Pending
* Confirmed
* Cancelled
* Failed

## Important Information

Users should ensure that the passenger and flight details are correct before completing the booking process.
