# Payment

## Payment Process

Payment is completed as part of the booking process.

After creating a booking, users can proceed with payment to complete their booking.

## Payment Methods

The system supports different payment methods, including:

* UPI
* Card
* Net Banking
* Wallet

## Payment Confirmation

A booking is confirmed after the payment process is completed and the payment is successfully verified.

## Successful Payment

After a successful payment:

* The payment is marked as successful.
* The booking can be confirmed.
* The selected seats can be confirmed.

## Failed Payment

If a payment fails, the payment is not considered successful and the booking is not confirmed through that payment.

Eligible failed payments may be retried.

## Payment Information

Payment information is associated with a booking and may include payment status and transaction-related details.

## Important Information

A successfully completed payment cannot be processed again for the same booking.
