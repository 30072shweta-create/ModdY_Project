# Pricing and Coupons - Frequently Asked Questions

## How does flight pricing work?

Flight pricing can consider the base fare, taxes, additional fees, and applicable discounts.

## Does the price vary by cabin class?

Yes. Flight pricing may vary depending on the selected cabin class.

## What cabin classes are available?

The system supports Economy, Premium Economy, Business, and First Class.

## How do coupons work?

Eligible coupons can be applied to receive a discount on a booking.

## What types of coupon discounts are available?

Coupons can provide percentage-based discounts or fixed amount discounts.

## What conditions apply to a coupon?

A coupon may have conditions related to its active status, validity period, minimum booking amount, usage limits, and maximum discount amount.

## How is the final price calculated?

The final price is calculated after considering applicable pricing, fees, and eligible discounts.
