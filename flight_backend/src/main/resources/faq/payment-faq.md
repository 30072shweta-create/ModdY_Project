# Payment - Frequently Asked Questions

## How does the payment process work?

After creating a booking, you can proceed with payment to complete the booking process.

## What payment methods are supported?

The system supports payment methods including UPI, Card, Net Banking, and Wallet.

## When is my booking confirmed?

A booking can be confirmed after the payment process is completed and successfully verified.

## What happens after a successful payment?

After successful payment, the payment is marked as successful, and the booking can proceed to confirmation.

## What happens if my payment fails?

If a payment fails, the payment is not considered successful and the booking is not confirmed through that payment.

## Can I retry a failed payment?

Eligible failed payments may be retried.

## Can I pay again for a successfully completed payment?

No. A successfully completed payment should not be processed again for the same booking.
