# Booking - Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I create a booking?

You can create a booking by selecting a suitable flight and providing the required passenger details.

## What information is required for a booking?

The booking process requires the selected flight and the required passenger information.

## Can a booking include multiple passengers?

Yes. A booking can include one or more passengers.

## When is a booking confirmed?

A booking is confirmed after the required booking and payment process is completed successfully.

## What information does a booking contain?

A booking can contain information such as the booking reference, selected flight, passenger details, booking status, and payment information.

## What happens if my booking is not completed?

The booking must successfully complete the required process, including payment where applicable, before it is confirmed.
