# Cancellation and Refund - Frequently Asked Questions

## How can I cancel my booking?

Eligible bookings can be cancelled according to the system's cancellation rules.

## What are the cancellation charges?

Cancellation charges depend on the time remaining before the flight departure.

More than 24 hours before departure: 10% cancellation charge.

Between 6 and 24 hours before departure: 25% cancellation charge.

Less than 6 hours before departure: 50% cancellation charge.

## What happens after I cancel my booking?

After an eligible booking is cancelled, the refund process is initiated.

## How is the refund amount calculated?

The refund amount is calculated after applying the applicable cancellation charges.

## Does cancellation depend on the flight departure time?

Yes. Cancellation eligibility and charges can depend on the booking status and the time remaining before departure.
