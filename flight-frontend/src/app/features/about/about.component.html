Our Story & Journey

Building the Airline We Always Wished Existed

Established in 2026, Meridian Airlines began with a clear premise: eliminate the stress, hidden fees, and complexity from modern air travel through relentless innovation, modern engineering, and heartfelt hospitality.

From our flagship operations hub at Kempegowda International Airport (BLR), we have expanded to serve major financial capitals and holiday destinations across Europe, the Middle East, North America, and Southeast Asia.
